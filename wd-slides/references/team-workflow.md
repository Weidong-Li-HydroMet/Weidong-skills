# Multi-agent team mode

Use when the user selects Team mode or Auto mode calls for parallel work. Use subagents within the current task; do not create separate user-owned chats merely to staff the team. Keep the chosen output format, scientific scope, and review-only boundaries unchanged.

## Team structure

A coordinator maintains the shared brief, narrative, work assignments, revision status, and final deliverables. It may also take one of the four core roles:

1. **Researchers:** read primary sources and select key original plots. Return source links/versions, figure identifiers, supported claims, numerical details, and limitations. Divide a large bibliography by scientific question or paper group, with distinct assignments.
2. **Builders:** turn verified evidence into editable slides with necessary text/math and APA footers. For larger decks, assign disjoint sections to several builders and designate one integrator to assemble the master deck and keep the story and styling consistent.
3. **Science reviewers:** independently compare actual renders and notes against primary sources for numerical accuracy, claim support, comparison fairness, and logic. Do not approve from a builder's summary alone.
4. **WD visual reviewers:** inspect actual rendered slides for typography, figure readability, layout, writing, citations, and consistency with the visual standards.

Four roles do not limit the team to four agents. Add researchers or section builders/reviewers when there is independent work to assign. Honor the user's requested headcount as far as the available tools allow. If concurrency is limited, schedule work in waves and explain the actual allocation; never claim more agents ran concurrently than actually did. If delegation is unavailable, disclose that limitation and use the same roles as sequential passes, without claiming independent review.

## Coordinate the work

- Give every agent the same brief: user questions, audience, scope exclusions, output format, approximate slide budget, and relevant skill references. Share a source registry and slide outline so parallel sections use compatible definitions and notation.
- Assign clear file or slide ownership. Researchers prepare separate evidence packets; section builders work in separate files or disjoint ranges. Only the designated integrator edits the master deck at a time. Reviewers report findings rather than silently overwriting the builder's work.
- Parallelize independent research and section drafting. Start source review as evidence arrives and rendered-slide review as completed batches become available. Do not draft numerical claims ahead of source verification or approve slides before the figures and text exist.
- Reconcile conflicting evidence through the primary source and stated assumptions. Resolve inconsistent definitions and citations before integration. Consensus among agents is not a substitute for evidence.

## Revision loop and delivery

Science and visual reviewers return specific findings with slide numbers, required corrections, and source references where relevant. The coordinator routes them to the responsible builder, then sends the repaired slides back for re-review. Repeat until material issues are resolved or clearly documented as limitations; do not call a blocked or unverified claim approved.

The integrator checks the complete narrative and closing synthesis, exports the deck, and arranges final render inspection. Review the actual final revision; recheck changed slides and any global changes affecting the others. Deliver one coherent deck and concise completion note, rather than separate unmerged agent outputs. Routine internal fixes do not need renewed user permission.
