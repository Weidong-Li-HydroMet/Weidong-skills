# The user's existing slide standards

Source: `wd-slides/SKILL.md`, read when this skill was created (2026-10-18). This file preserves the requirements relevant to slide review and talk preparation so that `wd-talk` can operate independently. These are the user's existing working standards. Unless supported by another public source, do not call them personal preferences established from Pedram's own talks. The user's explicit requirements for the current task take priority.

## Visual design and readability

- Use Avenir; black bold titles and blue subtitles; scientific figures as the dominant content; white backgrounds, alignment, and whitespace.
- Limit newly added emphasis to dark red and blue; the defaults are blue `#4285FF` and dark red `#8B0000`. Black, white, and gray may be used for body text, axes, and structure. Preserve the encodings and numerical meaning of existing scientific figures; do not alter original evidence merely to match a palette.
- Default to 16:9 for new decks. Preserve the dimensions of existing decks unless the user asks to change them.
- Adjustable starting font sizes: titles 36 pt, subtitles 28 pt, body text 22 pt, figure labels/legends 18 pt, and secondary sources 14 pt. These are production defaults, not exact sizes specified by the user or measured Pedram habits.
- Do not fill slides with decorative blocks, gradients, or unrelated images, or shrink body text beyond readability when projected. For unreadable figures, first enlarge, simplify, or split them across slides.
- When delivering a finished deck, verify that the target application actually renders Avenir. If it is unavailable, explain the limitation and resolve a substitute font with the user. Successfully setting a font name does not establish successful rendering.

## Existing scientific communication requirements

1. Give each slide one main message. Titles for established findings should state a supported conclusion; planning slides should clearly state an objective or test, never a completed finding.
2. Keep method colors, symbols, and line styles consistent in new figures. Preserve the original legends of figures from different papers and explain their differences.
3. Generally aim for no more than about six figure panels per slide. Split dense slides or move material to an appendix. This is a starting point, not a substitute for judgment about a specific slide.
4. Check axes, units, ticks, legends, annotations, and panel labels at the final display size.
5. Place limitations that affect the main conclusion on the relevant slide in brief, readable text. Secondary qualifications may go in the notes.

The figure, title, subtitle, and spoken explanation must support the same claim. Define unfamiliar metrics. Distinguish observation, interpretation, hypothesis, and plan, and distinguish correlation from causation. Identify missing baselines, controls, or uncertainty; never fabricate evidence to fill the gap.

Organize literature-review talks around scientific questions and prioritize figures from the original papers. On evidence slides, identify the source figure/panel and include an APA citation. Close by summarizing which questions have been answered and which remain open. Do not add the user's own results to a pure literature review unless requested.

## Boundaries between production and review

For review-only requests, deliver specific revision suggestions; do not automatically create or rewrite the deck. For production, prioritize the user's specified format. For a new deck without a specified format, retain the original skill's default of editable Google Slides. If access to the required service is insufficient, first complete the parts that can be produced and explain the specific limitation; do not switch cloud services without authorization.

Check the finished deck slide by slide for scientific and visual quality in the selected application or an available export that is clearly identified. Verify fonts, cropping, overlap, figure resolution, and slide order. If only local inspection was performed, do not claim that cloud import was verified. For multi-agent production, separate scientific review from visual review and recheck the final revised deck.

These requirements do not imply that Pedram has followed every rule in every public talk. To study his actual practice, consult `style-evidence.md`; do not use these standards as circular evidence for themselves.
