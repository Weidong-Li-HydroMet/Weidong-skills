# WD Skills

Five English-language skills for scientific work: paper reading, research discussion, presentation design, talk rehearsal, and manuscript writing.

| Display name | Folder and invocation | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WD-slides** | `wd-slides` / `$wd-slides` | Create, edit, and review scientific slides using the user's visual and scientific standards. |
| **WD-sci** | `wd-sci` / `$wd-sci` | Discuss research questions, papers, claims, and experiments using dated public evidence. |
| **WD-talk** | `wd-talk` / `$wd-talk` | Review slide arguments and rehearse a talk with one adaptive question at a time. |
| **WD-writing** | `wd-writing` / `$wd-writing` | Improve manuscript structure and prose while preserving scientific meaning and attribution. |
| **WD-reading** | `wd-reading` / `$wd-reading` | Read research papers in quick, normal, or detailed mode, with evidence, reproducibility checks, and transfer analysis. |

The visible names use `WD-*`; the canonical skill identifiers use lowercase `wd-*` for compatibility with the skill format. Each folder is self-contained and includes its own `SKILL.md` and supporting resources. Install the desired folders in your assistant's supported skills directory. Use the invocation above or let the assistant select a relevant skill automatically.

## Evidence and scope

The science, talk, and writing skills draw on public work by Pedram Hassanzadeh and his collaborators. Their source registries record dates, versions, authorship, reading coverage, and limits. Recent relevant evidence takes priority, with personal attribution distinguished from the strength of a scientific result. The current selected evidence corpus was reviewed on 2026-10-18 and includes instructions for refreshing relevant sources.

WD-reading preserves the user's supplied paper-reading workflow and its three modes. It organizes evidence without assigning a verdict or score and keeps its cited reading and reproducibility guidance in `wd-reading/SKILL.md`.

These skills are an evidence-informed research assistant, not an official representation of Pedram Hassanzadeh or a claim to reproduce his private views. Generated questions and revisions are not new evidence of his preferences. The slide-design standards are the user's requirements and are kept separate from observations of public talks.

All skill text is in English. Discussion, manuscript, and presentation language follow the user's request. The source registries link to public publications and talks; raw papers, recordings, private feedback, and local research logs are not bundled. The included slide image is a visual style reference, not evidence for a new scientific claim.
